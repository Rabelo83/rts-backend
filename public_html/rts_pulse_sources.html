RTS Pulse — Data Sources & Methodology

How each number on the live ridership board is calculated, and where the historical figures come from.

Current Time & Right Now Live

Pulled directly from RTS's BusTime real-time vehicle feed, refreshed continuously. "Right Now" is an estimate built from each active bus's reported occupancy band — BusTime only reports EMPTY, HALF_EMPTY, or FULL per vehicle, never an exact passenger count. Estimated values used: EMPTY = 5, HALF_EMPTY = 18, FULL = 36 riders per bus.

Today, This Week, This Month Estimated

Calculated by sampling the live occupancy estimate above every few minutes throughout the day, then multiplying the average riders-per-active-bus by the number of scheduled GTFS trips completed so far that day. This Week and This Month are simple sums of each day's calculated total.

This is an order-of-magnitude estimate, not an automatic passenger counter (APC) boarding count — a rider who boards partway through a trip or exits early isn't counted the same way as one who rides the full route. It resets daily and builds up over the course of each day rather than being available instantly at midnight.

Riders Since RTS Began Estimated — reconstructed historical total

RTS Gainesville became a public transit system in April 1974. There is no single official lifetime ridership figure — this board's number is a reconstruction built from confirmed annual figures found in historical and archived records, anchored at the end of FY2025 (September 30, 2025) plus the current fiscal year's total from the row above.

Working range: approximately 271,000,000 – 281,000,000 passenger trips through FY2025. The board displays a rounded figure (currently 275,000,000+) rather than a precise number, since the underlying reconstruction is not yet final.

Known anchor points used in this reconstruction:

  • 1974 — RTS becomes a public transit system (City of Gainesville / Alachua County)
  • 1977 — annual ridership exceeds 3,000,000 passenger trips
  • 1996 — annual ridership approximately 2,000,000
  • FY2012 — 10,698,984  ·  FY2015 — 10,293,434  ·  FY2019 — 9,212,465
  • FY2020 — 5,601,437  ·  FY2021 — 2,920,478 (pandemic-era decline)
  • FY2023 — ~5,170,533  ·  FY2024 — ~5,529,000  ·  FY2025 — ~4,978,991
  • RTS's own 50th-anniversary materials (2024) report approximately 202.4 million riders over the preceding 25 years

1974–1994 records are not fully available as clean annual figures; that period is currently estimated at roughly 50–60 million passenger trips based on available operating history. This figure will be refined as further archival research is completed, and the baseline will be updated without changing how the counter works.
